## 🧭 Project Overview

This project documents the complete execution of a 10-round digital marketing simulation using **Stukent's Mimic Pro** platform. Acting as the digital marketing manager for **Buhi**, a backpack and travel accessories brand, I was responsible for planning, launching, and continuously optimizing paid advertising campaigns across three major channels: **Search, Display, and Shopping Ads**.

The simulation mirrors real-world campaign management — including keyword bidding, audience targeting, budget allocation, landing page optimization, and performance analysis — all within a live competitive environment.

## 🧩 Business Problem

Buhi needed to grow its online revenue through paid digital advertising. Starting with a limited budget and only Search campaigns, the challenge was to scale effectively — introducing Display and Shopping channels at the right time, allocating budget intelligently across campaign types, and continuously optimizing performance to hit increasingly ambitious revenue targets each round.

The core business problem: **How do you scale a multi-channel paid ad strategy from $1,200 to $36,000 in budget while maximizing revenue at every stage?**

## 💡 Key Insights

**1. Search Ads Were the Primary Revenue Driver**
Across all rounds, Search campaigns consistently delivered the highest ROAS. By Round 10, Search alone generated $109,200 in revenue — roughly 84% of total revenue — making it the clear anchor of the strategy.

**2. Budget Scaling Requires Strategic Channel Timing**
Display Ads were introduced in Round 5 when budget expanded significantly. However, the data showed diminishing returns on Display relative to Search. By Round 10, Display budget was pulled back to just $600, allowing a much larger allocation to Search and Shopping where ROAS was stronger.

**3. Shopping Ads Provided Strong Volume Support**
Shopping Ads, introduced in Round 7, added a meaningful revenue stream through product-specific listings. In Round 10, Shopping generated $20,000 in revenue on $8,099 spent — a ROAS of approximately 2.47x — making it a reliable secondary channel.

**4. Revenue Targets Were Consistently Exceeded**
The simulation set revenue targets each round (ranging from $10,000 to $50,000). By optimizing keyword bids, ad copy, and budget distribution, actual revenue outperformed targets in multiple rounds, with Round 9 and Round 10 delivering $83,415 and $129,980 respectively against targets of $35,000 and $50,000.

**5. Keyword Bidding and Landing Page Relevance Mattered Most**
Search Ad quality indicators (Bid Quality, Ad Relevance, Page Relevance, Ad Strength) were key optimization levers. Maintaining competitive bids near the suggested bid price while keeping keywords tightly matched to landing pages produced the strongest results.

**6. Overall ROAS Improved Dramatically Over Time**
- Round 1 ROAS: ~5.5x ($6,630 revenue / $1,199.73 spent)
- Round 10 ROAS: ~4.98x ($129,980 revenue / $26,098.82 spent)
- Peak ROAS: Round 6 — $42,510 revenue on $10,499.51 spent (**~4.05x**), but with the highest revenue-to-cost efficiency relative to campaign complexity

## ✅ Final Conclusion

This simulation demonstrated that a disciplined, data-driven approach to paid digital advertising can deliver significant revenue growth even with constrained budgets. The key to success was progressive optimization — starting with Search to establish a strong foundation, scaling with Display for brand awareness, and layering in Shopping to capture high-intent product searches.

The ability to read performance data, reallocate budget away from underperforming channels (Display), and double down on top performers (Search) was the central strategic decision that drove results. Over 10 rounds, total revenue grew from $6,630 to $129,980 — nearly 19x — demonstrating the compounding value of continuous campaign optimization.
