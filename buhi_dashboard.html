Insight 01

Search was the primary revenue engine throughout

Search Ads generated $109,200 of the $129,980 final round revenue — 84% of total. Strong keyword relevance and competitive bidding kept ROAS at 6.28x.

Insight 02

Display was pulled back as data showed lower returns

Display budget was cut from $9,000 (R5) to just $600 (R10) as ROAS data consistently showed it underperforming Search. Smart reallocation drove efficiency.

Insight 03

Shopping Ads added reliable product-level revenue

Launched in R7, Shopping Ads generated $20,000 on $8,099 spent in Round 10 — a 2.47x ROAS — providing strong support alongside the dominant Search channel.

Insight 04

Revenue targets were consistently exceeded

Round 10 target was $50,000. Actual revenue hit $129,980 — exceeding the target by 160% through aggressive optimization and smart budget allocation.

Insight 05

Revenue grew 1,860% from Round 1 to Round 10

Starting at $6,630 in Round 1 with just $1,200 in budget, the strategy scaled to $129,980 in Round 10 with a $36,000 budget — demonstrating compounding optimization gains.

Insight 06

Landing page relevance was a key quality driver

Ad Relevance and Page Relevance indicators guided keyword-to-landing-page alignment. Keeping these scores high reduced wasted spend and improved conversion rates.
